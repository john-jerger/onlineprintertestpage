What CMYK means

CMYK stands for Cyan, Magenta, Yellow and Key (black), the four inks used by most color printers. Each should print as a solid, vivid block.

Important accuracy note

Web pages cannot send true CMYK values. This page is drawn in RGB and converted by your printer driver. It reliably shows a faded or blocked ink, but it is not for exact color matching or professional proofing.

Pinpointing the failing ink

Faded cyan weakens greens and blues. Faded magenta weakens reds and pinks. Faded yellow makes oranges and greens look cold. Weak black makes text look gray.
